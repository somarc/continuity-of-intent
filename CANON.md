# Continuity of Intent

**Status:** Founding edition  
**Form:** Public, amendable canon  
**Purpose:** Preserve the human through-line in AI-assisted engineering

## Preamble

Models and contexts are transient. Possibilities multiply faster than any one
person could produce them by hand. Yet possibility is not direction, and
generation is not responsibility.

In sustained AI-assisted work, the human author is the durable state: the
person who carries purpose across sessions, chooses among plausible futures,
reconciles discoveries with prior commitments, and remains answerable for what
becomes real.

Continuity of intent is the discipline of making that work explicit.

## Article I — Authorship

Authorship is a sustained obligation:

1. **Sustain intent.** Hold the work's purpose through changes in tools,
   contributors, implementation, and understanding.
2. **Select possibilities.** Choose among plausible outputs according to
   reasons that can be stated, challenged, and revised.
3. **Remain responsible.** Stand behind the consequences of the selected work,
   including its omissions, uncertainty, and need for correction.

Authorship does not require producing every artifact by hand. It requires
being answerable for the whole.

## Article II — Continuity

Continuity is not stasis. A coherent project must change when evidence,
conditions, or understanding require it.

Continuity is preserved when change is conscious, coherent, and legible:

- **Then:** what the work meant and what was believed;
- **Learned:** what evidence or constraint changed understanding;
- **Now:** what is deliberately retained, revised, or retired.

An amendment that names its reasons can preserve intent more honestly than an
unchanged implementation whose original purpose has been forgotten.

## Article III — The Intent Constitution

A repository should externalize commitments that cannot safely remain
implicit. Its Intent Constitution records:

1. **Purpose** — what the work is for.
2. **Commitments** — what must remain true.
3. **Boundaries** — what the work will not claim or do.
4. **Terms** — what its important words mean.
5. **Evidence** — what has actually been demonstrated.
6. **Gaps** — what remains uncertain, incomplete, or unverified.
7. **Amendments** — how the work changes without losing its thread.

Code records execution. The Constitution records why.

## Article IV — Evidence and intent

Intent and evidence must remain adjacent, but neither may impersonate the
other.

- **Intent** states the property, purpose, invariant, or outcome the work is
  designed to establish.
- **Demonstrated** states only what has been observed, reproduced, measured,
  inspected, or independently established.
- **Open** names what remains to be verified, bounded, repaired, or
  strengthened.
- **Limits** state the boundary of the review or evidence.

Respect is precise. Honest review neither inflates aspiration into proof nor
uses incompleteness to erase originality and substantial engineering value.

## Article V — Provenance

This canon emerged from a sustained, single-human-author engineering effort
developed with extensive assistance from multiple generations of AI systems.
The work consumed billions of model tokens during a period of rapid change in
AI-assisted software engineering.

The models contributed research, implementation proposals, critique, and
iteration. Continuity of intent, architectural selection, reconciliation, and
responsibility remained human obligations.

Oak Chain made that obligation impossible to ignore. Across changing models,
context windows, branches, and years of iteration, the human author was the
only component with durable state.

## Amendment protocol

This canon may change. Every material amendment should record:

- the prior statement;
- what was learned;
- the revised statement;
- what continuity is preserved;
- the evidence or decision record supporting the change.

The goal is not to freeze language. The goal is to prevent context loss from
silently making decisions on our behalf.

## Closing declaration

Tools will change. Models will be replaced. Context will be lost and
recovered.

The work remains authored when its intent is carried forward consciously, its
changes can be explained, and someone remains responsible for the whole.

**Keep the thread.**
