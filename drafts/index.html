Canon for responsible authorship in AI-assisted engineering

The human is the durable state.

In multi-model engineering, context passes, systems change, and possibilities multiply. Authorship remains with the person who sustains intent, chooses among those possibilities, and accepts responsibility for what becomes real.

01 / The condition

Possibility is not direction.

A model can generate a path. It cannot own the destination. It can continue a prompt, transform a file, or propose an answer. It cannot, by itself, maintain the meaning of a project across changing contexts, competing options, and consequences not yet visible. Where continuity matters, someone must carry it.

You were the only component with durable state.

Across changing models, context windows, branches, and years of iteration, the human author remained the semantic control plane: remembering what the work was for, recognizing plausible but wrong trajectories, and reconciling every discovery with the whole.

02 / The canon

Authorship is a sustained obligation.

In AI-assisted engineering, authorship is not measured by keystrokes. It is the continuous act of carrying purpose, selecting among possible futures, and remaining answerable for the result.

01

Sustain intent.

Hold the work's purpose through changes in tools, contributors, implementation, and understanding.

02

Select possibilities.

Choose among plausible outputs according to reasons that can be stated, challenged, and revised.

03

Remain responsible.

Stand behind the consequences of the selected work, including its omissions, uncertainty, and need for correction.

Authorship does not require producing every artifact by hand. It requires being answerable for the whole.

03 / Conscious change

Continuity is not stasis.

A coherent project must change when evidence, conditions, or understanding require it. Continuity is the discipline of making that change conscious, coherent, and legible.

Then

State the prior intent.

Record what the work meant to accomplish and what was believed when the earlier decision was made.

Learned

Name what changed.

Identify the evidence, constraint, failure, or new understanding that made revision necessary.

Now

Choose deliberately.

Make clear what is retained, revised, or retired—and which deeper continuity the change preserves.

04 / The repository as public memory

Code records execution. The Constitution records why.

A repository should externalize commitments that cannot safely remain implicit. This is its Intent Constitution: a revision instrument that preserves reasons without freezing the work.

01

Purpose

What is this work for?

02

03

Boundaries

04

06

07

Amendments

How does the work change without losing its thread?

05 / The review method

Intent is not evidence. Evidence is not the whole intent.

A responsible review keeps these statements adjacent without allowing either to impersonate the other.

Intent

The purpose, property, invariant, or outcome the work is designed to establish.

Demonstrated

Only what has been observed, reproduced, measured, inspected, or independently established.

Open

What remains to be verified, bounded, repaired, consolidated, or strengthened.

Limits

The boundary of the evidence, review, environment, and claim.

Respect is precise. Candor is not a verdict on originality; it is how original engineering is described truthfully.

06 / Provenance

One author. Many models. One continuous obligation.

This canon emerged from a sustained, single-human-author engineering effort developed with extensive assistance from multiple generations of AI systems. The work consumed billions of model tokens during a period of rapid change in AI-assisted software engineering.

The models contributed research, implementation proposals, critique, and iteration. Continuity of intent, architectural selection, reconciliation, and responsibility remained human obligations.

Oak Chain made that obligation impossible to ignore.

Its history deserves two records: the archaeology of how the mountain was climbed, and the evidence-backed integration of what now deserves to stand. Explore the originating branch.

Keep the thread.

Tools will change. Models will be replaced. Context will be lost and recovered. The work remains authored when its intent is carried forward consciously, its changes can be explained, and someone remains responsible for the whole.
